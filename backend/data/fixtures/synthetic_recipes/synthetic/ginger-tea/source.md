# Ginger Tea

A warming two-cup infusion of fresh ginger and honey. Serves 2.

Prep time: 5 minutes. Cook time: 10 minutes. Total time: 15 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 2 inches fresh ginger, thinly sliced
- 2 tbsp honey
- 4 cups water

## Method

1. Bring the water to a boil, add the sliced ginger, and simmer uncovered for
   10 minutes.
2. Strain into mugs, stir in the honey while hot, and serve immediately.
