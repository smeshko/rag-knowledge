# White Bean Soup

A cozy, weeknight soup of creamy white beans, sweet onion, and vegetable
stock. Serves 4.

Prep time: 15 minutes. Cook time: 30 minutes. Total time: 45 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 1 onion, diced
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 2 cups white beans, cooked
- 4 cups vegetable stock

## Method

1. Warm the olive oil in a heavy pot and soften the diced onion over medium
   heat until translucent, about 8 minutes.
2. Add the white beans and vegetable stock, bring to a gentle simmer, and cook
   for 20 minutes so the flavours come together.
3. Blend half the soup until smooth, stir it back in, season to taste, and
   serve hot.
